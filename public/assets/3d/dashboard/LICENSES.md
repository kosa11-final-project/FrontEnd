# Dashboard 3D asset licenses

The 3D models in this directory are selected from the following Kenney asset packs.

- City Kit (Industrial): https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial
- Car Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit

Both packs are distributed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0.
Attribution is not required, but the source is retained here for asset provenance.

The parcel models in `online/` are selected from the following pack.

- Factory Kit 3.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/factory-kit

The pack is distributed by Kenney under Creative Commons CC0 1.0.

The department-store buildings in `downtown/` are selected from the following pack.

- Downtown City MegaKit (Standard FREE): https://quaternius.com

The pack is distributed by Quaternius under Creative Commons CC0 1.0.
